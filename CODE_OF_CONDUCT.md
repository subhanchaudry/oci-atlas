# OCI Atlas community conduct

OCI Atlas welcomes people learning, operating, and improving Oracle Cloud guidance. Treat contributors and users with respect, regardless of background, identity, experience, or technical familiarity.

- Discuss the proposal, evidence, and technical tradeoffs. Critique ideas without attacking people.
- Help newcomers with clear, constructive explanations. Credit other people's work and preserve required attribution.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project. Do not post harassment, threats, discriminatory content, spam, or other people's private information.
- Share sanitized examples. Credentials, customer data, and private infrastructure details do not belong in public discussions.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close disruptive discussions, and restrict participation when necessary. Responses should be proportionate to the behavior and its effect on the community.

For abusive or threatening content, use GitHub's **Report content** or **Report abuse** controls. See [GitHub's reporting guidance](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam). Avoid repeating private information in a public report. For a security issue affecting OCI Atlas, follow the private reporting route in [Support](SUPPORT.md).

These standards apply to this repository's issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project discussions. This is an original community policy maintained by OCI Atlas contributors.
